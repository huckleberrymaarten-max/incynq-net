Before you ask

This isn't another
Primfeed.

Primfeed is a great platform — but it's essentially Twitter for Second Life. A social feed where everyone posts everything. InCynq was built to solve a different problem entirely.

Primfeed

  • Open social feed — everyone posts everything
  • Follow people and see all their posts
  • No interest filtering
  • No inworld integration
  • No brand ad platform
  • Multiple virtual worlds
VS

InCynq

  • Interest-filtered feed — you choose what you see
  • Follow brands and only see content you asked for
  • Interest groups filter everything
  • Inworld Terminals, ATMs and direct TP links
  • Targeted ad platform built for SL brands
  • Second Life only — built for one world, done right

Different tool. Different purpose. Use both if you want — but InCynq is the one built specifically to fix the noise.
