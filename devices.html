InCynq Terminal (ICQ-T)

The activation point. Walk up to a Terminal inworld, pay the one-time activation fee, and your InCynq account is linked to your Second Life avatar instantly. No browser, no copy-pasting codes. Just tap, pay, done.

The activation fee is returned in full as credit to your InCynq wallet — so you're not paying to join, you're funding your account from the start.

Terminals are placed in participating venues across the grid. Sim owners can apply to host a Terminal — they earn a share of every transaction made at their location.

InCynq ATM

The wallet top-up point. Pay L$ at any InCynq ATM inworld and the credit appears in your InCynq wallet within seconds. Use it to boost an event you've created, or spend on upcoming features.

ATMs are available as freestanding units and wall-mounted versions. Clean, simple, and always available when you need to top up.

InCynq Terminal

Activate your account
inworld.

Already signed up on incynq.app? Link your avatar to your account at any Terminal. Takes less than a minute.

  1. 1
    Create your account first Sign up at incynq.app using your SL avatar username. Free, takes two minutes.
  2. 2
    Find an InCynq Terminal inworld Look for the ICQ-T terminal at any participating venue. Use the SLURLs below to teleport directly.
  3. 3
    Touch the Terminal and pay Tap the Terminal, confirm the activation fee in L$, and pay. Your account is activated instantly — and the full amount is returned as credit to your InCynq wallet.
  4. 4
    You're in Your InCynq account is now fully active. Head to incynq.app and start following the brands you love.

InCynq ATM

Top up your wallet
inworld.

Need more credit for ads or upcoming features? Any InCynq ATM puts L$ straight into your InCynq wallet.

  1. 1
    Find an InCynq ATM inworld Available at participating venues. Use the SLURLs below to teleport directly to one.
  2. 2
    Touch the ATM A menu appears with top-up amounts. Select the amount you want to add.
  3. 3
    Pay in L$ Confirm the SL payment prompt. L$ are non-refundable once paid.
  4. 4
    Credit appears instantly Your InCynq wallet is topped up within seconds. Ready to use for event boosts and upcoming features.
