Install InCynq

No app store. No waiting. Just add InCynq to your home screen and open it like a native app — full screen, fast, always there.

Step by step

Choose your device.

🤖
Android
Use Chrome — recommended
1
Open incynq.app in Chrome Chrome gives you the best experience — full screen, no URL bar, feels exactly like a native app.
2
Tap the three-dot menu The ⋮ icon is in the top-right corner of Chrome.
3
Tap "Add to Home screen" You'll see this option in the dropdown menu.
4
Tap "Add" to confirm InCynq appears on your home screen with its own icon. Open it — no browser chrome, no URL bar.
Firefox on Android also works, but shows a URL bar at the bottom of the screen. This is a browser limitation — Chrome removes it completely.
🍎
iPhone & iPad
Use Safari — required
1
Open incynq.app in Safari On iOS, only Safari can add apps to your home screen. Chrome on iOS cannot do this.
2
Tap the Share button The Share icon (□↑) is at the bottom of Safari on iPhone, or at the top on iPad.
3
Tap "Add to Home Screen" Scroll down the share sheet until you see this option.
4
Tap "Add" in the top right InCynq appears on your home screen. Open it for the full-screen experience.
Must use Safari on iOS. Chrome, Firefox and other browsers on iPhone and iPad cannot add apps to the home screen — this is an Apple restriction.
