Everything you need to know about InCynq — for residents and brands.

For residents

Getting Started

What is InCynq?▾
InCynq is a social platform built for Second Life residents. It solves the group chat spam problem — instead of noisy SL group messages, you follow the brands and people you care about and only see content that matches your interests.
Does InCynq use an algorithm?▾
No. InCynq has no algorithm. Your feed shows content from the people and brands you follow, in chronological order. No hidden ranking, no shadowbanning, no 'we think you might like this'. What you follow is exactly what you see — nothing more, nothing less.
Is InCynq free for members?▾
Yes, completely free. No subscription, no paywall, no catch. You will see sponsored posts from brands, but only if they match your chosen interests. Members never pay anything to register or use the platform.
Do I need a Second Life account to join?▾
Yes. A valid SL account is required to register. This is how we confirm you are a real person and not a fake or bot account. Your SL avatar name becomes your InCynq username.
How do I sign in?▾
Use your SL avatar name and your InCynq password. Your InCynq password is separate from your SL password — InCynq never asks for your SL password.
Does InCynq need my Second Life password?▾
Never. InCynq has its own separate password. We will never ask for your SL password. If anyone claiming to be InCynq asks for it, it is a scam.
How do I activate my account after registering?▾
After registering you will see a Pending Activation screen. Visit an InCynq terminal inworld in Second Life and tap it. The terminal confirms it is really you. Come back to the app and your account activates automatically.
What is the minimum age?▾
16 — the same as Second Life. Adult-rated location ads require you to be 18 or over and SL adult-verified.
Do I get any welcome credit when I join?▾
Yes. Every new member receives 100 L$ welcome credit on activation. This can be used to boost your first event. It is non-transferable and never expires.

Your Profile

What name shows on my profile?▾
Your SL display name by default. If it differs from your SL username, both are shown — display name large, @username smaller below it. You can switch to showing your username only in Settings → Show Display Name.
Can I use SL special characters in my name and posts?▾
Yes. InCynq has a built-in SL character picker — tap the ★ button in any text field. It includes decorative borders, name deco, Japanese characters, symbols, and emoji — everything people commonly use in SL names and posts.
Can people find me in search?▾
Yes, by default. Turn off Appear in Discovery in Settings if you want to stay off the radar. Your posts are still visible to your followers either way.
How do I change my interests?▾
Go to your profile and scroll to Your Interests. Tap any interest group to toggle it on or off. Subcategories expand when you select a group. Your feed updates immediately.
Can I upload a profile photo?▾
Yes. In Edit Profile you can upload your own photo or tap Use SL Picture to automatically fetch your current Second Life profile picture.
What are the rules for profile pictures?▾
Your profile picture is visible to everyone across the entire platform regardless of maturity settings. Explicit, nude, or sexually suggestive content is strictly prohibited in profile pictures — no exceptions, regardless of your adult verification status. Clean avatars, SL portraits, and personal photos are all fine.
What is the Discover section on my profile?▾
It shows members and brands that share your interest groups. It updates as you add or change your interests. You can follow anyone directly from the Discover cards.
How do I see who I am following?▾
Tap the Following count on your profile. A sheet slides up showing everyone you follow. InCynq Official is always at the top and cannot be unfollowed — it is how InCynq communicates with you.

The Feed

Why don't I see posts from everyone?▾
By design. InCynq only shows you posts from people and brands you follow, matched to your interest groups. No algorithm deciding what is popular — just the things you chose to follow.
Why am I seeing sponsored posts?▾
Brands pay to appear in feeds. You only ever see sponsored posts if the brand's chosen interest groups match yours. No random ads — only things relevant to your SL interests.
What does 'Based on your interests' mean?▾
It means that sponsored post was targeted to one of your interest groups specifically. It is there because it matched what you told InCynq you care about.
How do I post?▾
Tap the + button in the top navigation bar. Write your caption, optionally add a photo, pick tags from your interest groups, and tap Post.
Can I post links or SLurls?▾
No. Links and SLurls are not allowed in regular posts — this keeps the feed spam-free. Brands can include teleport links and Marketplace links in paid ads only.
Can I post explicit or adult content?▾
No. InCynq is not an adult content platform. Explicit, nude, or sexually suggestive content is not permitted in posts under any circumstances. Adult verification does not change this. Any explicit post will be removed immediately and the account suspended.
Can I save posts?▾
Yes. Tap the bookmark icon on any post to save it. The icon turns green when saved.
What does 'Out of This World' mean as a report reason?▾
Content that has nothing to do with Second Life — real life content that doesn't belong on a platform for the SL community. You can report it and we will review it manually.
What happens when I report a post?▾
The post is immediately auto-hidden for you and goes to our manual review queue. We review every single report ourselves. It is completely anonymous.

Events & Pings

Can I post events for free?▾
Yes. Creating and posting events is completely free for all members and brands. You only pay if you want to boost your event for more visibility.
How does event boosting work?▾
On your event, tap Boost. Choose a tier (⚡ Basic, ⭐ Featured, or 👑 Premium) and the number of days you want to boost. The cost is deducted from your InCynq wallet. If your balance is too low, you will be prompted to top up inworld.
What are the event boost prices?▾
⚡ Basic: 100 L$/day. ⭐ Featured: 250 L$/day. 👑 Premium: 500 L$/day. You can boost for 1, 2, or 3 days.
What are Pings?▾
InCynq's name for direct messages. Private one-to-one conversations between members. Coming soon — tap the 💬 icon in the top navigation when available.

Member Wallet

Do members have a wallet?▾
Yes. Every member has an InCynq wallet. It starts with 100 L$ welcome credit when you activate your account.
What can I use my wallet for as a member?▾
Boosting your own events. That is it — members cannot place feed ads. If you want the full advertising suite you need to upgrade to a brand account.
How do I top up my wallet?▾
Find an InCynq ATM inworld in Second Life and follow the instructions. Your wallet updates automatically.
Are wallet credits refundable?▾
No. All L$ deposited to your InCynq wallet is non-refundable. It has no cash value and cannot be transferred to another account. Only top up what you plan to spend.
Does the welcome credit expire?▾
No. Your 100 L$ welcome credit never expires.
How does the referral program work?▾
Share your unique referral link from your profile. When a friend signs up using your link and activates their account (taps an InCynq terminal inworld), you earn 10 L$ credited directly to your wallet.
Where do I find my referral link?▾
On your profile, tap the 'Invite Friends' button. Your unique referral link and stats are shown there. Tap Copy to share it.
How many friends can I refer?▾
Unlimited. Share your link as much as you like. However, you can only earn rewards for the first 10 successful referrals per calendar month.
What counts as a successful referral?▾
A successful referral is when someone signs up using your link AND activates their account by tapping an InCynq terminal inworld. You are paid 10 L$ when they activate, not when they register.
When does the referral limit reset?▾
On the 1st of each month. If you reach 10 paid referrals in January, you can earn 10 more in February, and so on.
Can I refer myself or use multiple accounts?▾
No. Self-referrals and creating fake accounts to abuse the referral system will result in permanent account suspension with no wallet refund.

Safety & Privacy

How does InCynq verify members?▾
Every member must have a valid SL account and activate inworld via an InCynq terminal. The terminal reads your SL avatar identity directly. No anonymous accounts, no fake profiles.
What are InCynq's Community Standards?▾
InCynq is for everyone. Hate speech, antisemitism, racism, Islamophobia, homophobia, transphobia, extremist ideology, harassment, threats, and content that glorifies violence are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate suspension or permanent ban. We don't tolerate it in any form. If you see it — report it.
Is InCynq moderated?▾
Yes, actively. Every post is checked automatically before it appears in the feed. Flagged content goes to a human review queue and is reviewed promptly. We do not wait for reports — we look. Every report is also reviewed manually by the InCynq team. Nothing happens to an account automatically — a human makes every decision.
What content is completely banned?▾
Anything involving minors, hate speech of any kind (including antisemitism, racism, homophobia, transphobia, extremism), harassment, impersonation, spam, explicit content, and scams. Zero tolerance — permanent ban and reported to authorities where required by law.
What is InCynq's adult content policy?▾
InCynq is not an adult content platform. Adult verification only unlocks adult-rated location advertisements and SIM listings. It does not permit explicit photos, posts, or profile pictures anywhere on InCynq. This is absolute — no exceptions.
How do maturity levels work?▾
You can select one or more levels in Settings → Maturity Level. General is always on and cannot be turned off. Moderate can be toggled freely. Adult requires SL adult verification and confirmation you are 18 or over. You see content from all levels you have enabled.
Are suspensions automatic?▾
No. Every suspension is a manual decision by the InCynq team after reviewing a report. Reports auto-hide content but nothing else happens until a human makes a call.
Does InCynq sell my data?▾
Never. InCynq is operated from Ireland under EU GDPR. We collect only what is needed to run the platform. We do not sell, share, or trade your personal data with anyone.
Does InCynq track me?▾
Members — no. Your posts, profile visits, and activity as a member are not tracked. We only track engagement with brand content (so brands can see how their posts perform). When you view a brand post or visit a brand profile, an anonymous count is logged — the brand only sees aggregate numbers, never your individual identity.
What exactly gets tracked for brand content?▾
When you scroll past a brand post in your feed (impression), open its comments (view), or visit a brand profile, we log the event. We record the post, a timestamp, and your user ID — but the brand only ever sees aggregate counts in their dashboard, not a list of who you are.
Can brands see who viewed their content?▾
No. Brands see how many people viewed — total and unique — but not which specific users. Individual viewer identities are never exposed in the brand dashboard. This is enforced at the database level.
Can I opt out of brand analytics tracking?▾
It is applied at the interaction level, not the user level, because the brand dashboard depends on it working for all viewers. If you object, email legal@incynq.net and we'll discuss options under GDPR.
How do I delete my account?▾
Go to your profile → tap the ⚙️ settings icon → scroll to Danger Zone → Delete Account. You have a 14-day cool-off period to change your mind — a banner will show in the app with a Cancel option. After the cool-off ends, everything is permanently deleted. This fulfils your right to erasure under GDPR.

Grid Status

What is Grid Status?▾
Your online status as shown on InCynq. Three options: 🟢 In-world (visible to everyone), 🟡 Friends only (visible to mutual follows only), or ⚫ Hidden (always shows as offline).
How do I change my Grid Status?▾
On your profile, tap the status text under your name. A sheet slides up with the three options. Tap to change instantly.
Can I hide my status completely?▾
Yes. Set Grid Status to Hidden and you will always appear offline to everyone on InCynq.

Email & Notifications

What emails does InCynq send?▾
Only what you choose. Account emails (activation, important notices) always come through. Everything else — InCynq News, Tips & Guides, Special Offers — is optional and toggleable in Settings.
Does InCynq show me ads from outside companies?▾
No. The only sponsored content you see comes from Second Life brands within InCynq, matched to your own chosen interest groups. No third-party advertising. Ever.
What are push notifications?▾
Push notifications are alerts that appear on your phone or device even when you're not in the app. InCynq sends them for a small number of meaningful events only — new events from brands you follow, event reminders when you've RSVP'd, new followers, and important announcements from InCynq Official.
How do I turn push notifications on or off?▾
Settings → Push Notifications. One toggle — on or off. That's it. InCynq keeps notifications minimal by design — you'll never be spammed.
Can I turn off push notifications but still see in-app notifications?▾
Yes. Turning off push notifications only stops alerts appearing on your device. Your in-app notification bell still shows everything as normal.
Can I install InCynq on my phone like a real app?▾
Yes! InCynq is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — you can add it to your home screen and it launches fullscreen just like a native app. On Android, open incynq.app in Chrome, tap the three-dot menu and select 'Add to Home Screen'. For the best experience — including full-screen mode with no browser bar — we recommend using Chrome. Firefox works too but may show a small URL bar at the bottom.
Does InCynq work on iPhone?▾
Yes. Open incynq.app in Safari on your iPhone, tap the Share button, then 'Add to Home Screen'. It will install as a PWA and launch fullscreen from your home screen.

For brands

Setting Up Your Brand Account

What do I need before creating a brand account?▾
A member account on InCynq. Brand accounts are always linked to a personal member account. You manage both from one login.
How do I create a brand account?▾
Settings → Add Brand Account. Fill in your brand name, description, logo, and brand email. Pay the 3,500 L$ activation fee using an InCynq ATM inworld. That 3,500 L$ goes directly into your Brand Wallet as ad credit — nothing is wasted.
Can I have more than one brand account?▾
Not in the current version. One member account, one brand account, linked together.
How do I switch between my personal and brand account?▾
Tap your avatar in the top navigation to open the account switcher. Tap any account to switch instantly — no logout needed.
What is the Brand Team feature?▾
You can invite one Manager to help run your brand account. A manager can post, create events, boost, manage ads, top up the wallet, and reply to comments. They cannot delete the brand, invite others, or transfer ownership.
How do I invite a manager?▾
Settings → Brand Team → Invite a Manager. Enter their SL avatar name. They get a notification and can accept. You can cancel the invite or remove a manager at any time.
Does a manager use their own wallet to place ads?▾
No. All spending by a manager — ads, event boosts, and any other L$ charges — is deducted from the brand owner's Brand Wallet. The manager's own InCynq wallet is never touched.
Can a manager resign?▾
Yes. A manager can step down at any time from their brand profile. The brand owner receives a notification immediately.

Advertising

How does InCynq advertising work?▾
You create location ads that appear in the feed and explore for members who match your chosen interest groups. Ads run for 7 days. Members only see your ad if they have joined at least one of the groups you targeted.
What are the ad tiers?▾
Three ad plans, four pricing tiers. Plans: Basic (highlighted in search and explore), Featured (injected in feed), Premium (top story + feed + explore). Base rates per 7 days for 1 group — Launch Tier (under 1,000 members): Basic 150 / Featured 400 / Premium 800 L$. Growth Tier (1,000-5,000): Basic 250 / Featured 750 / Premium 1,500 L$. Established Tier (5,000-15,000): Basic 400 / Featured 1,200 / Premium 2,500 L$. Premium Tier (15,000+): Basic 600 / Featured 1,800 / Premium 3,500 L$. Current tier always shown in the Advertise section. Reach estimates are shown once the platform reaches a sufficient number of members and update automatically as InCynq grows.
How does interest group targeting work?▾
You pick which interest groups see your ad. Members only see it if they have joined at least one of your chosen groups. Pricing scales with the number of groups: 1 group = base price, 2 groups = 1.8×, 3 groups = 2.5×, 4 or more groups = 3× (capped).
What is Random Rotation?▾
When you select 2 or more interest groups, you can enable Random Rotation. Your ad rotates through your selected groups randomly instead of targeting all simultaneously. This costs 25% less. Good for broad reach on a tighter budget.
Can I run adult ads?▾
Only if your brand account has SL adult verification and targets verified adult members only. Adult ads may promote adult-rated SIM locations and services. Explicit imagery in ads is not permitted regardless of maturity level. Running adult ads to non-adult audiences results in immediate account suspension.
Will ad prices increase?▾
Yes. Pricing adjusts automatically based on the size of the InCynq community — as we grow, everyone moves to the next tier together. The current tier and pricing are always visible in the Advertise section of the app. Brands that join early benefit from lower tiers until the community reaches the next threshold.
How does my brand grow on InCynq if there are no reposts or shares?▾
InCynq is built around targeted reach, not viral mechanics — and that is intentional. Your brand grows through five channels: targeted ads that put you in front of members who already care about your niche, appearing in Discover for members who share your interest groups, your follower base who see every post you make, the Cynqified badge that builds trust across the whole community, and boosted events that highlight what you have inworld. Word of mouth happens naturally on SL's own channels. InCynq's job is to make sure the right people find you in the first place.
Can I get a refund on ad spend?▾
No. Once L$ is charged for an ad, it cannot be reversed. The no-refunds notice is shown clearly at every payment step before you confirm.

Brand Wallet

What is the Brand Wallet?▾
A L$ balance held on InCynq, used for running ads, boosting events, and optional paid features like the Dashboard Upgrade. It is created automatically when you activate your brand account. The 3,500 L$ activation fee becomes your opening balance.
How do I top up my Brand Wallet?▾
In the Advertise tab, tap Top Up. Choose an amount, receive a unique payment code, find an InCynq ATM inworld and follow the instructions. Your wallet updates automatically.
Are wallet balances refundable?▾
No. All wallet L$ is non-refundable. This applies to activation fees, ad spend, event boosts, Cynqified application fees, and Dashboard Upgrade subscriptions. Only top up what you plan to spend.
What happens to my wallet if my account is suspended?▾
Your Brand Wallet is frozen. No ads can run, the Dashboard Upgrade pauses, and no new payments can be made until the suspension is lifted. If the account is permanently banned, the wallet balance is forfeited.

Events & Boosting

Can brands post events?▾
Yes. Event creation is free for brands and members alike. Brands can post events for their locations, product launches, parties, live sets — anything inworld.
How does event boosting work?▾
Tap Boost on your event. Choose a tier (⚡ Basic at 100 L$/day, ⭐ Featured at 250 L$/day, or 👑 Premium at 500 L$/day) and the number of days (1, 2, or 3). L$ is deducted from your Brand Wallet. If your balance is insufficient, the top-up flow opens automatically.
Can I use SL characters in event titles?▾
Yes. Tap the ★ button next to the event title field to open the SL character picker. Add decorative characters, borders, and symbols just like you would in SL itself.

Cynqified Verification

What is the Cynqified badge?▾
The ⚡ Cynqified badge means InCynq has manually checked an account and confirmed it's genuinely who it says it is. It shows on the profile, on posts, and on ads.
Who can be verified?▾
Brands and DJ / Live Performers. It's about a commercial identity — confirming this really is that shop, or that performer. A performer pays the same as a brand, because a stage name is a commercial identity too.
I have a brand and a performer identity — does one cover the other?▾
No, they're checked separately. Each one is its own identity with its own name, so each gets looked at on its own.
How do I apply?▾
Settings → Get Cynqified. Fill in your SL location or Marketplace URL, a description of your brand, and how long you have been in Second Life. The 1,500 L$ fee is charged on submission. We review within 24–48 hours.
What do you actually check?▾
Three things, and we check them in Second Life rather than from behind a desk. First, are you trading — is there a store inworld or a Marketplace shop with real stock in it? Second, is it yours — does the store owner match the account, and do the name, logo and products line up? Third, are you passing yourself off as someone else. Sharing a name with another brand is fine; looking like them isn't.
Someone else already uses my brand name — can I still get verified?▾
Usually yes. Two brands can genuinely share a name, and if you each have your own look and your own customers we're happy to verify both. What we won't do is verify a brand that could be mistaken for an existing one — same name and a similar logo or style is a different matter.
Do I need to have been around for years?▾
No. A brand new brand with a proper store and its own look is exactly what we're looking for. We're asking whether a real business exists, not how old it is.
What is the fee for, exactly?▾
The check itself. We go inworld, look at your store, open your Marketplace shop and see how you're trading — and that's the same work whether the answer turns out to be yes or no. That's why it's charged when you apply, and why it isn't refunded either way.
What if you turn me down?▾
We'll tell you why, and in most cases it's something you can fix — a store that wasn't set up yet, or a look that's too close to another brand. You're welcome to apply again once it's sorted.
What does Cynqified mean to members?▾
It tells them your brand has been manually checked by InCynq and is the real deal. It is not an endorsement of your products or services — just confirmation that you are who you say you are.
Can the badge be removed?▾
Yes. If your brand breaches InCynq's Terms & Conditions, the badge can be revoked. Serious breaches also result in suspension or permanent ban.

Dashboard

What is the Dashboard?▾
Your brand's analytics hub. It shows how many people have seen your posts, visited your profile, and followed you. Every brand account gets a free basic dashboard as part of being a brand — no extra setup needed.
Where do I find the Dashboard?▾
Open your profile. You'll see a Dashboard button (📊) between Invite Friends and Edit Profile. Tap it.
What does the free Dashboard include?▾
Last 30 days of: total post views, impressions, unique viewers, profile visits, new followers, total followers. A 7-day view trend chart. Your top 3 posts by views. Everything you need to know how your content is landing.
What is the Dashboard Upgrade?▾
A paid add-on that unlocks deeper insights: engagement rates, view-through rates, peak viewing hours, best day of week, 90-day extended stats, full post rankings (not just top 3), and audience behaviour data. 250 L$ per month, billed from your Brand Wallet.
How do I upgrade my Dashboard?▾
Open the Dashboard. Scroll to the bottom and tap 'Upgrade for 250 L$/month →'. Confirm the charge and you're upgraded instantly. The first 30 days start from that moment.
How does auto-renewal work?▾
Every 30 days we automatically charge 250 L$ from your Brand Wallet. You'll get a heads-up notification 7 days before renewal, another at 3 days, and a confirmation on the day it charges.
What if my wallet is empty on renewal day?▾
Your upgrade enters a 3-day grace period. The upgraded features pause but nothing is lost — your data is kept in full. Top up your wallet during those 3 days and the subscription resumes automatically. If grace expires without payment, the subscription is marked expired (but again — data is preserved, you can resubscribe anytime).
Can I cancel my Dashboard Upgrade?▾
Yes, anytime. Your upgrade stays active until the end of the current 30-day cycle — you're not charged again. No prorated refunds, so if you cancel on day 29 you still have one more day of upgraded access.
Can I get a refund on a 250 L$ charge?▾
No. Once the charge goes through, it is not refundable. This includes accidental upgrades. Make sure you want the upgrade before confirming — we show your wallet balance and the amount before you commit.
Will the Dashboard Upgrade price change?▾
Possibly. We'll give 30 days' notice before any change. Existing subscribers move to the new rate from their next renewal — you always know before you're charged.
Can other brands see my analytics?▾
No. Your analytics are yours alone. Other brands, other members, and the public see nothing. Only you (and anyone you've invited as a Brand Team manager) can see your Dashboard.
Does the InCynq official account pay for the Dashboard?▾
No. The official InCynq account has permanent free access to the full Dashboard — no billing, no renewal cycle. It's how InCynq monitors its own presence on the platform.

Safety & Enforcement for Brands

What happens if my brand is reported?▾
Reports go to our manual review queue. Content is auto-hidden pending review. We review every report ourselves. Nothing happens to your account automatically — a human makes every decision.
What counts as a serious breach for a brand?▾
Adult ads shown to non-adults, explicit content in ads or posts, harassment, hate speech, fraud, impersonation, and anything involving minors. These are treated seriously from the first offence.
What is the suspension process?▾
First serious offence: immediate suspension, Brand Wallet frozen, all ads paused, Dashboard Upgrade paused. You can appeal at legal@incynq.net within 14 days. Second offence or extremely severe first offence: permanent ban, wallet forfeited, no appeal.
Can I appeal a suspension?▾
Yes. Contact legal@incynq.net with your brand name and the reason you believe the suspension was issued in error. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
Are there automated suspensions?▾
No. We do not auto-suspend anyone based on report counts or algorithms. Every action is a manual decision made by the InCynq team.

Brand Email & Notifications

What emails do brand accounts receive?▾
Account emails (suspension notices, billing confirmations) always come through. Optional emails include: Ad Performance Reports (weekly reach summary) and Billing & Wallet (top-up reminders, Dashboard renewal reminders). Both are on by default and individually toggleable in Settings.
Can I turn off billing emails?▾
Yes. Settings → Email → Billing & Wallet. Toggle it off. Just be aware you won't get top-up reminders if your wallet runs low or Dashboard renewal reminders.
Where do Dashboard renewal reminders go?▾
Both in-app (as a system notification in your bell) and optionally by email if Billing & Wallet emails are on. You get one 7 days before renewal, one 3 days before, and a confirmation on the day it charges.

Your Links

Where do I add my store, Marketplace and website links?▾
In Edit Brand Profile. Add them once and they're there for every ad you run — no retyping. They show on your brand profile too, so they're worth filling in even if you never advertise.
Can I link to my website from an ad?▾
Yes. Add it in Edit Brand Profile and we'll check it before it goes live — usually a day or two. After that you can use it as a button on any ad.
Why do you check website links?▾
A SLurl or a Marketplace link can only ever take someone to Second Life — we can see that from the address itself. A website could go anywhere, so a real person has a look first. It's the only way we can promise residents that a button on InCynq is safe to tap.
Why was my website turned down?▾
We'll tell you why on the Edit Brand Profile screen, so you can fix it and try again. The usual reasons: it doesn't load, it isn't https, it's a link shortener, it's not Second Life related, or it isn't yours.
I changed my website and now it says pending again — why?▾
Every website gets checked, including a new one replacing an old one. Your previous website keeps working on your ads while we look at the new one, so nothing goes dark in between.
How many links can one ad have?▾
Up to two, picked from the ones you've saved. One clear destination usually works better than two.

Running Your Ads

Can I pause an ad?▾
Yes. Pausing stops it being shown and keeps the days you've paid for — start it again whenever you like and it picks up where it left off. You can bank up to 30 days that way.
What happens if I delete a running ad?▾
It stops straight away and the days left on it are gone — there's no refund. If you only want to stop it for a while, pause it instead and you keep everything.
Why don't you refund the unused days instead?▾
Because longer bookings are discounted — four weeks costs about half the weekly rate. Refunding unused weeks would make cancelling cheaper than booking a single week properly. Pausing gives you the time back instead, which is fairer all round.
How do I know if anyone saw my ad?▾
Every ad shows how many times it was seen, how many people saw it, and how it's done in the last 7 days. It's on the ad card in the Advertise section.

For DJs & performers

Getting Started

What is a DJ / Live Performer account?▾
It's your own identity on InCynq, separate from your personal account. Your stage name, your @handle, your followers, your own Performer Wallet and your own tip jar. You post as the artist, not as yourself — and when you go live, your followers hear about it.
How much does it cost to become a DJ or performer?▾
1,750 L$ to activate. That's not a fee that disappears — the whole amount lands in your Performer Wallet, which at 175 L$ an hour is your first 10 hours on air.
How do I set one up?▾
In the app, add a DJ / Live Performer the same way you'd add a brand. Fill in your stage name, what you do, your genres and your stream URL, then pay the activation at an InCynq ATM inworld. Your identity goes live as soon as the payment lands.
Do I need to activate at a Terminal first?▾
Yes, and this one matters. Your payouts go to the Second Life avatar captured when you activate at a Terminal — that's how we know which avatar to send your tips to. No Terminal activation, no avatar on file, and we have nothing to pay into. Do it before your first gig, not after.
Can I have a DJ identity AND a brand?▾
Not on one slot. Every account comes with one brand-type identity, and that slot can be a brand or a performer. If you already run a brand and want a DJ identity too, get in touch via the contact form and we'll add a second slot for you. It's free, it's just not self-service — we do it by hand.
Can I change what's on my performer profile?▾
Yes, any time, free. Your bio, genres, stream URL, home SLurl, sample link, photo and performer type are all editable from your performer dashboard. The one thing you can't change yourself is your stage name — see 'Changing your stage name' below.

Airtime

What is airtime?▾
Broadcasting time. You hold a balance of hours and you spend them by going live. Activation gives you 10 hours to start, and you top up whenever you need more at 175 L$ an hour.
How is airtime actually charged?▾
By the minute, rounded up, for the time you were genuinely on air. You don't book a slot or declare a set length in advance — you go live, you play, you end the set, and only what you used comes off your balance.
What happens if I run out mid-set?▾
You can't start a set without airtime, and a session is capped at the hours you're holding — so it'll end rather than run up a bill you didn't agree to. Top up before a long night and you'll never meet the cap.
What if my connection drops?▾
You're billed to your last heartbeat, not to whenever we noticed. If your stream or your app dies mid-set, the session closes itself and charges what you actually broadcast. You don't pay for a set you weren't playing.
Can I get a refund on my Performer Wallet?▾
No. Your Performer Wallet is non-refundable, like every InCynq wallet — it buys airtime and promotion, not a balance you can cash out. Only top up what you intend to use.
What else does my Performer Wallet pay for?▾
Promotion for your gigs — event boosts and ads, the same options brands have. It's one spend balance for everything on the performer side.

Going Live

How do I go live?▾
Create your event, add your stream URL, and tap Go Live when it's time. Your stream plays inside InCynq, a 🔴 LIVE badge appears on your profile and your gig, and your followers get a notification that you're on air.
Do I need my own stream?▾
Yes. Bring your own stream URL — whatever you already use to broadcast in Second Life. InCynq plays it to your audience; it doesn't host it for you.
Who sees that I'm live?▾
Your followers see you in their feed, because that's their feed and you're someone they chose. Everyone else finds you in Events, where every live gig is listed — they can listen there and follow you from it. That's how a new listener becomes a follower.
Can I see who's listening?▾
Yes. You get a live listener count while you play, and your peak and total listeners are kept on the session afterwards, so you can see how a gig actually went.
Does my stream URL get exposed?▾
No. The events list never returns it. It's handed to signed-in listeners only, and only while you're actually live.
What happens when I end the set?▾
Airtime stops, your gig moves to your history, and the tip window on that gig closes out in the normal way. Ended events clear themselves off the board after 24 hours.

Tips

How do tips work?▾
Anyone listening can tip you from their InCynq wallet while you're live. It lands in your tip earnings — which is a different pot from your Performer Wallet, and the only money that ever leaves InCynq as real L$.
Can someone tip more than once in a gig?▾
Yes, as often as they like. A tip jar gets hit several times a night in Second Life and we weren't going to cap that at one. There's a short cooldown between tips to stop accidental double-taps, and that's all.
What does InCynq take?▾
5% of each tip, as a handling fee. It covers holding your tips, working out what you're owed and getting it to your avatar. We take it on tips only — never on your airtime, never on your activation, never on anything you top up.
Why can't a friend tip me with their welcome credit?▾
Because we gave them that credit — nobody paid it in. If promotional credit could be tipped, InCynq would be funding your tips out of its own pocket, which isn't a tip. Welcome credit still buys everything else: boosts, ads, airtime. Tips have to be money someone actually put in.
Are my Performer Wallet and my tip earnings the same balance?▾
No, and they never mix. Your Performer Wallet is what you spend on InCynq — airtime, boosts, ads — and it's non-refundable. Tip earnings are what your audience gave you, and that's yours to be paid out. One goes in, the other comes out.

Getting Paid

When do I get paid?▾
Each tip is held for 7 days, then goes into the next payout run. Payouts go out weekly — one payment per DJ, covering everything that's cleared. Play three nights in a week and you get one transfer, not three.
Why hold tips for 7 days?▾
It's the window where a disputed gig can still be sorted out while a refund is possible. After it closes, the money is yours and on its way.
How does the money reach me?▾
As real L$ to your Second Life avatar, sent from the IncynqPayments avatar inworld. Second Life doesn't let a website push Linden dollars on its own — someone has to send them — so a person at InCynq pays each run by hand and records it. At our size that's a feature: every payment is looked at by a human.
Which avatar gets paid?▾
The avatar that activated the account at a Terminal, by its UUID rather than its name. Display names change; UUIDs don't. It means there's never a question about which DJ Max was meant.
Where can I see what I'm owed?▾
On your performer profile. You can see tips still inside their hold window, what's cleared and due in the next run, and every payment you've already had.

Changing Your Name & Leaving

Can I change my stage name?▾
Yes — DJs rebrand, that's normal. It costs the same as activating, 1,750 L$. Get in touch through the contact page and pick 'DJ / Live Performer'; we'll set up a payment code for you, and the name changes once it's paid.
What happens to my followers if I rebrand?▾
You keep everything — followers, posts, gig history and your Performer Wallet. Only the name changes, and your @handle updates to match it. Old links to your previous handle stop working, which is the trade-off for a clean rebrand.
Does the rebrand fee go into my Performer Wallet?▾
No — that's the one difference from activation. Activation puts the full amount into your Performer Wallet. A rebrand is just a fee for the work.
Can a brand rebrand too?▾
No. A brand is a brand. Rebranding is a DJ and performer thing, because stage names genuinely change.
If I delete my account, do I lose the tips I've earned?▾
No. Deleting has a cool-off period that's longer than the 7-day tip hold, so everything you've earned is paid out to your avatar during it, before the account actually closes. You never lose money you earned by leaving.
What happens to my Performer Wallet if I delete?▾
It's not refunded — spend credit never is. If what you really want is a different name, rebranding keeps your credit and your following, so it's almost always the better move.
